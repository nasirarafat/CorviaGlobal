About Corvia Global

An independent partnership architect for international education

Many education partnerships stall because the right institutions never meet, or because ambition runs ahead of regulation, funding and delivery capacity. Corvia Global exists to close that gap.

We assess markets and partners, design partnership models that fit both sides, and support delivery through small, well-evidenced pilots. Participating institutions keep their independence and decision-making throughout.

How we work

A clear path from idea to pilot

Three stages with decision points, so partners commit only when evidence, funding and approvals are in place.

  1. Discover

    Needs assessment with institutions, faculty, learners and employers. Shortlist priorities and suitable UK partners.

  2. Design

    Build the pilot plan with scope, costs, responsibilities and risk controls. Confirm interest in writing and check regulatory requirements.

  3. Deliver and evaluate

    Run the approved pilot, measure against a baseline and publish a short evaluation to decide whether to stop, adapt or scale.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you a recruitment agency?

No. Corvia Global advises on institutional partnerships. We are not paid per student recruited and do not act as a student recruitment agent.

Can you arrange UK degrees to be delivered overseas?

We assess whether joint, dual or transnational awards are feasible. Any such arrangement depends on the approvals of the UK university and the regulators in each country, and nothing is promised before they are in place.

How are programmes funded?

Funding is worked out after scope and partner interest are clear. Options include institutional contributions, eligible public schemes, employer sponsorship and philanthropic support. No funding is assumed secured.

How do we get started?

Send us a short note about your institution and what you hope to achieve. We will arrange an exploratory conversation and agree whether a scoping phase makes sense.
